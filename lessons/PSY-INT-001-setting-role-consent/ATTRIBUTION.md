## Jurisdictional scope

Legal and regulatory requirements for consent, confidentiality, records, and observer presence differ by jurisdiction and by institution. This lesson cites Canadian professional guidance, United States statutory summaries, a German documentation study, and a Portuguese survey, and it says so in the prose each time. Nothing in this pack states a universal legal rule, and no reader should treat any statement in it as the law of their own jurisdiction.
